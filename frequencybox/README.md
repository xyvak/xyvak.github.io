# Frequency Box (جعبه ابزار فرکانس)

**Frequency Box** is a comprehensive acoustic utility application for Android devices. It provides a suite of 10 professional audio tools designed for everyday users, mobile technicians, and audio enthusiasts. 

The application is highly optimized and focuses on real-time audio synthesis and acoustic physics.

## 🌟 Core Features

### 1. Water Eject & Speaker Cleaner (تخلیه آب اسپیکر)
A scientifically proven method to remove trapped water and dust from smartphone speakers using Acoustic Resonance. The app utilizes a 3-step automated algorithm:
* **Deep Vibration:** Sustained 165Hz sine wave to maximize diaphragm displacement.
* **Frequency Sweep:** Rapid transitions from low to high frequencies to guide liquids out.
* **Shockwave Pulses:** Short, high-amplitude bursts to eject remaining droplets.

### 2. Audio Frequency Generator (ژنراتور فرکانس)
Real-time signal generator capable of producing frequencies from 1Hz to 22,000Hz with 1Hz precision. Supported waveforms include:
* Sine Wave (سینوسی)
* Square Wave (مربعی)
* Sawtooth Wave (دندانه‌اره‌ای)
* Triangle Wave (مثلثی)

### 3. Ultrasonic & Animal Whistle (امواج فراصوت)
Generates high-frequency sounds (above 20kHz) that are beyond human hearing but highly perceptible to animals. Useful for dog training (Dog Whistle) and testing ultrasonic responses.

### 4. Hearing Test (تست شنوایی)
Evaluates the user's hearing threshold across different frequency spectrums to estimate physiological ear age.

## 💎 Transparency & Accessibility
We believe in providing accessible tools. Out of the 10 modules available in the application, **9 tools are completely free to use** (including Water Eject, Frequency Generator, and Hearing Test). Only the "Mental Health & Meditation" module requires an in-app purchase.
